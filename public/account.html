    How paying works

    The same on every product

    You pay for each action when you do it: publishing a site, starting a desktop, and so on. The product opens a payment window, your wallet signs one transfer for exactly that amount, and the action goes ahead.

    There is no balance to top up and nothing renews. A payment covers the one action it was made for.
